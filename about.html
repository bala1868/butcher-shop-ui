OUR ROOTS

Small counter.
Big standards.

Gobi Butcher Shop started with a simple frustration: finding meat that was halal, responsibly raised, and genuinely fresh shouldn't be a treasure hunt.

So we opened our doors, built relationships with farmers who share our values, and brought skilled butchers back to the heart of the neighbourhood. Today, every cut still passes through careful hands before it reaches your kitchen.
